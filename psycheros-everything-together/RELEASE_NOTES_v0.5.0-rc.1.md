# Everything Together 0.5.0-rc.1

Rebased the combined source bridge onto stock Psycheros 1.0.2. More Uploads,
Expression Sprites, and Screen Presence are merged once across the new action,
Flux, voice, and agent-loop architecture. The suite also carries the verified
1.x-compatible Accessibility, Windows Shell, and HTF manager-plugin packages.

The guarded installer accepts only pristine 1.0.2 files or this identical
payload, creates timestamped backups, and refuses unknown local edits.
